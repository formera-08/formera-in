02 Studio

We shape motion, light and form.

Formera Creations is an independent visual design studio crafting motion graphics, brand films and 3D-driven identities for people who refuse to look ordinary.

We started as a small creative practice posting experiments online and grew into a studio that designs complete visual worlds — from the first frame of a teaser to the last beat of a campaign.

Every project begins with a form, a rhythm, and an idea worth moving. We combine art direction, animation and post-production under one roof so your story stays consistent everywhere it appears.
