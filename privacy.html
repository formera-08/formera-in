Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026. Your privacy matters to us. This page explains, in plain language, how Formera Creations handles your information when you visit this site or get in touch with the studio.

01What we collect

This website does not use contact forms, accounts, cookies for tracking, or analytics that identify you. The only information we receive is what you choose to send us yourself — for example when you email the studio, call us, or message us on Instagram.

02How we use it

Details you share with us (such as your name, email address, phone number, and project brief) are used only to respond to your enquiry, plan and deliver creative work, and manage our working relationship. We do not sell, rent, or trade your information with anyone.

03Third-party services

Links on this site may take you to third-party services such as Instagram or a booking platform (e.g. Topmate). Those services have their own privacy policies, and anything you share with them is governed by their terms, not ours.

04Data retention

We keep project-related communication only as long as needed to complete the work and meet any legal or accounting obligations, after which it is deleted.

05Your rights

You can ask us at any time to see, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. Just email the studio and we will take care of it.

06Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, the revised version will be published on this page. Continued use of the site after an update means you accept the revised policy.
